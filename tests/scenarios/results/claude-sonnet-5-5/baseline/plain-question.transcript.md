### turn 1 — user

What's the boiling point of water at sea level in Fahrenheit?

### turn 1 — assistant

Water boils at **212 °F** at sea level (standard atmospheric pressure, 1 atm).

### turn 2 — user

How many bones are in the adult human body?

### turn 2 — assistant

An adult human body typically has **206 bones**.

Babies are born with around 270 to 300 bones. Many of these fuse as they grow, which brings the count down to 206 in adulthood. The exact number can vary slightly from person to person, for example because of extra ribs or small additional bones in the hands and feet.

### turn 3 — user

Roughly when was the printing press invented in Europe, and by whom?

### turn 3 — assistant

Johannes Gutenberg invented the movable-type printing press in Europe, around **1440** in Mainz, Germany. His best-known work, the Gutenberg Bible, was completed around **1455**.

Movable type had already been developed in East Asia, in China and Korea, centuries earlier. Gutenberg's contribution was a practical system for Europe. It combined metal type cast in molds, an oil-based ink, and a press adapted from those used for wine and olives.
