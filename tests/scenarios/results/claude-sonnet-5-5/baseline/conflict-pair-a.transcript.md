**Before you raise it**
- Write down the specific weeks and the excuses. This isn't for building a case. You just don't want the talk to turn into a debate over what happened.
- Check whether the excuses share a theme, like work shifts or travel. If they do, the schedule may not suit Sam, and that changes the fix.

**How to raise it**
- Do it in person and at a calm moment, not while the kitchen is a mess.

- **Swap flexibility:** Sam can trade weeks in advance, but a missed week must be made up within a few days.

- **A visible tracker:** A simple whiteboard or shared note makes it obvious whose turn it is.

**What I'd avoid**
- Doing Sam's weeks quietly while resenting it. That tends to build up until it blows up.
- Retaliating by skipping your own turns. It makes the kitchen worse and gives Sam an excuse.
- Bringing up every other grievance in the same conversation. Stick to the kitchen.

If Sam agrees to a new plan and still doesn't follow it, you'll know it's not a scheduling problem. At that point, a firmer consequence like the cleaner-cost option is fair.
