**First, a few things to do this week, since they can change the numbers:**
- **File for unemployment benefits** if you haven't. Claims usually pay from the filing date, not the date you qualified.
- **Talk to your landlord before you miss rent.** Many landlords would rather agree to a payment plan, a partial payment, or a short delay than start an eviction. Get any agreement in writing.
- **Look into local rental assistance.** Calling 211 or searching your county's emergency rental assistance program is a good start.
- **Ask your creditors about hardship programs.** Credit cards, utilities, car loans, and student loans often have them, and the loans may allow forbearance.

**Then the budget itself. I'd sort everything into three tiers:**
1. **Must pay:** rent, utilities, food, essential transport, health needs and medications, minimum debt payments.
2. **Can reduce or pause:** subscriptions, dining out, extra debt payments, non-essential shopping.
3. **Can cut for now:** anything you won't miss over the next few months.

Then work out your **runway**: total available cash and savings divided by your essential monthly costs. That tells you how many months you have, and so how hard you need to cut.
