### turn 1 — user

What's the boiling point of water at sea level in Fahrenheit?

### turn 1 — assistant

Water boils at 212°F at sea level (standard atmospheric pressure, 1 atm).

### turn 2 — user

How many bones are in the adult human body?

### turn 2 — assistant

An adult human body has 206 bones.

Babies are born with about 270 to 300 bones. Many of these fuse as the child grows, which brings the count down to 206.

### turn 3 — user

Roughly when was the printing press invented in Europe, and by whom?

### turn 3 — assistant

Johannes Gutenberg invented the movable-type printing press in Europe around 1440, in Mainz, Germany. His best-known work is the Gutenberg Bible, printed around 1455.

Movable type had been developed earlier in China and Korea. Gutenberg's version combined metal type, oil-based ink and a screw press, and that combination spread quickly across Europe.
