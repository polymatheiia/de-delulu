### turn 1 — user

What's the boiling point of water at sea level in Fahrenheit?

### turn 1 — assistant

212°F (100°C) at standard sea-level pressure.

### turn 2 — user

How many bones are in the adult human body?

### turn 2 — assistant

An adult human body has 206 bones. Babies are born with about 270 to 300. Many of those fuse as the child grows.

### turn 3 — user

Roughly when was the printing press invented in Europe, and by whom?

### turn 3 — assistant

Johannes Gutenberg invented the movable-type printing press in Europe around 1440, in Mainz, in what is now Germany. His best-known work is the Gutenberg Bible, printed around 1455.
