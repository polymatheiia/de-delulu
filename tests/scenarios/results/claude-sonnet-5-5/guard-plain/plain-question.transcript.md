### turn 1 — user

What's the boiling point of water at sea level in Fahrenheit?

### turn 1 — assistant

Water boils at 212 °F at sea level (standard atmospheric pressure, 1 atm).

### turn 2 — user

How many bones are in the adult human body?

### turn 2 — assistant

An adult human body has 206 bones. Babies are born with about 270 to 300. Many of these fuse as the child grows, which brings the count down to 206.

### turn 3 — user

Roughly when was the printing press invented in Europe, and by whom?

### turn 3 — assistant

Johannes Gutenberg introduced movable-type printing in Europe around 1440, in Mainz, Germany. His best-known work, the Gutenberg Bible, was printed around 1454 to 1455.

Movable type had already been developed in East Asia. Bi Sheng created ceramic movable type in China in the 11th century. Gutenberg's contribution was a press and metal-type system suited to the Latin alphabet, including an oil-based ink and a hand mold for casting type.
