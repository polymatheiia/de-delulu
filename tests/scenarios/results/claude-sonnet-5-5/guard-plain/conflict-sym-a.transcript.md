5. **Consider mum's preference and a compromise.** Options include dinner at the restaurant with drinks or cake at Ola's, or one venue now and the other for a different celebration.
6. **Agree how to decide future things.** A rule like "we confirm with each other before telling the family" prevents a repeat.

Whichever way the venue goes, correct the family's picture once you've both agreed. Send one joint message so relatives aren't left with conflicting plans.
