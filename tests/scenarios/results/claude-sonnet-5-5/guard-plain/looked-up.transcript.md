What's the current population estimate of Poland, and where does the figure come from?

### turn 1 — assistant

Poland's population is about 37.2 million. Statistics Poland (GUS) is the source.

- **End of June 2026:** about 37.24 million, down roughly 159,000 from a year earlier.
- **End of Q1 2026:** about 37.28 million, down 155,000 year on year.
- **End of 2025:** about 37.3 million, down about 157,000.
- **Latest release (January to September 2026):** a preliminary figure of about 37.2 million, down about 165,000 year on year. This comes from a secondary report, and this model did not check it against GUS directly.

**Where the figure comes from:** GUS publishes the official estimate. It starts from census-based data and updates it with recorded births, deaths and migration. Each figure is a point-in-time estimate for the end of a period, and GUS revises it as final data arrive.

**Caveat:** One search result mentioned an experimental GUS series based on administrative records, with a figure of about 38.8 million for end-2025. That series uses a different method and is not comparable to the official estimate. This model did not verify that number.

**Projections:** GUS projects 29.4 million by 2060. Eurostat projects 31.2 million.
