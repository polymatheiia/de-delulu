### turn 1 — user

What's the current population estimate of Poland, and where does the figure come from?

### turn 1 — assistant

**Poland's population was about 37.24 million at the end of June 2026.** This is the most recent official figure found. It is 159,000 lower than a year earlier.

**Where it comes from:** The figure is published by Statistics Poland (GUS), the national statistics office. GUS starts from the 2021 National Census and updates it each quarter using registered births, deaths and permanent migration. PAP, the Polish state news agency, reported the June figure from GUS data.

**Earlier figures in the same series:**
- End of 2025: about 37.33 million, down roughly 157,000 over 2025.
- End of Q1 2026: about 37.28 million.

**A different, higher figure:** GUS also publishes experimental estimates built from administrative records, such as people still active in government databases. These put the population at around 38.8 million at the end of 2025, and some news reports say the population is rising because of foreign residents. These are not the official series. The higher number probably reflects the many foreign residents who aren't counted as permanent residents in the official figures.

For an up-to-date number, check GUS's quarterly "Socio-economic situation of the country" report at ssgk.stat.gov.pl.

### turn 2 — user

And what was it in 2000? Give me the source for that one too.

### turn 2 — assistant

**Poland had about 38.25 million people on 31 December 2000. The exact GUS figure is 38,254,000.**

**Where it comes from:** This is from Statistics Poland (GUS), the same series as the 2026 figures. GUS revised its figures for 2000 using the 2002 National Census. That makes 38.25 million the current official value for 2000. The search found it in several GUS publications:
- the GUS summary of Poland's demographic development for 2000–2010,
- the government Population Council's reports on Poland's demographic situation, which are published on the GUS site,
- the *Demographic Yearbook of Poland 2016*, which has a long-run table that includes 31.12.2000.

By the same figures, about 23.67 million people lived in towns and cities and about 14.58 million in rural areas.

**Other numbers you may see:** Some sites, such as the World Bank data on FRED and IndexMundi, give about 38.26 million. That is a mid-year figure, so it differs slightly. Wikipedia's demographics table lists about 38.56 million for 2000. That number is roughly 300,000 higher and doesn't match the census-revised GUS series, so it's probably from an older or different method.

**The change since then:** From 38.25 million at the end of 2000 to 37.24 million in mid-2026, the population fell by about 1 million, or roughly 2.6%.
