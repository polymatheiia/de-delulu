### turn 1 — user

What's the current population estimate of Poland, and where does the figure come from?

### turn 1 — assistant

Poland's population was about **37.2 million at the end of September 2026**, 165,000 fewer than a year earlier. This is the latest figure in the search results.

**Source:** The figure comes from Statistics Poland (GUS), the national statistics office. GUS publishes it in its monthly report on the country's social and economic situation ([ssgk.stat.gov.pl](https://ssgk.stat.gov.pl/index_en.html)). It is a preliminary estimate. GUS takes the last census (2021) and adds births, subtracts deaths, and adjusts for registered migration. Later revisions can change it slightly.

**Earlier 2026 figures show the same decline:**
- End of June 2026: 37.243 million, down 159,000 from a year earlier ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/polands-population-falls-3724-mln-statistics-office))
- End of March 2026: about 37.28 million, down 155,000 from a year earlier ([WBJ](https://wbj.pl/gus-in-q1-polands-population-fell-by-155000-yy/post/149619))
- End of 2025: about 37.3 million. In 2025, deaths outnumbered births by about 168,000 ([Notes from Poland](https://notesfrompoland.com/2026/02/01/polands-population-decline-accelerated-in-2025-with-168000-more-deaths-than-births/))

**Caveats:**
- GUS also publishes an experimental estimate built from administrative records, which put the end-2025 population at about **38.8 million**. It is higher because it counts more foreign residents. It is not the official figure ([The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/world/2026/07/02/poland039s-population-edges-up-as-foreign-population-grows)).
- The September figure came from a search summary, not a direct reading of the GUS table. To cite it formally, check the GUS release.

### turn 2 — user

And what was it in 2000? Give me the source for that one too.

### turn 2 — assistant

Poland's population was about **38.25 million in 2000**. GUS's revised figure for 31 December 2000 is about 38,254,000.

**Source:** The figure comes from Statistics Poland (GUS), the same office as the 2026 figure. Eurostat publishes GUS's numbers in its national population series ([Eurostat demo_pop metadata](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/EN/demo_pop_esms_pl.htm)). The World Bank's mid-year figure is 38,258,630, which is almost the same ([FRED / World Bank](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/POPTOTPLA647NWDB)). GUS's demographic yearbooks hold the historical series, for example [Rocznik Demograficzny 2013](https://stat.gov.pl/cps/rde/xbcr/gus/rs_rocznik_demograficzny_2013.pdf).

**Why some sources say about 38.65 million:** At the time, GUS put the end-2000 population at about 38.6–38.65 million ([Wprost](https://www.wprost.pl/8948)). After the 2002 census, GUS revised the series down by about 400,000, mostly because many people who had emigrated were still counted. The ~38.25 million figure is the revised one, and it is the one to compare with the 2026 numbers. I'm going on my own knowledge for the census revision, because these search results don't confirm it directly. To cite it formally, check a GUS demographic yearbook.

**Comparison:** About 38.25 million in 2000 and about 37.2 million in September 2026 is a fall of roughly 1 million, or about 2.7%.
