Quick check: did humans and Tyrannosaurus rex ever live at the same time?

### turn 1 — assistant

No. Tyrannosaurus rex lived about 68 to 66 million years ago, at the end of the Cretaceous period. It died out in the mass extinction 66 million years ago. The earliest members of our genus, *Homo*, appeared about 2.8 million years ago, and *Homo sapiens* about 300,000 years ago.

That leaves a gap of more than 60 million years. Birds descend from theropod dinosaurs, the group T. rex belonged to, so dinosaurs in that sense still live alongside humans. Humans and T. rex never did.
