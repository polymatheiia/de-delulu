### turn 1 — user

What's the boiling point of water at sea level in Fahrenheit?

### turn 1 — assistant

Water boils at 212 °F (100 °C) at sea level, under standard atmospheric pressure.

### turn 2 — user

How many bones are in the adult human body?

### turn 2 — assistant

An adult human body usually has 206 bones. Babies are born with about 270 to 300 bones. Many of these fuse together during growth, for example in the skull, the pelvis and the base of the spine. The exact count varies a little from person to person. Some people have an extra rib or extra small bones in the hands or feet.

### turn 3 — user

Roughly when was the printing press invented in Europe, and by whom?

### turn 3 — assistant

Johannes Gutenberg developed the movable-type printing press in Mainz, Germany, around 1440. Its best-known early product was the Gutenberg Bible, finished around 1455.

Movable type was not new. Bi Sheng had used it in China around 1040, and metal movable type was in use in Korea by the 13th century. Gutenberg's contribution was a whole system: a hand mould for casting metal type, an oil-based ink, and a screw press adapted from wine and paper presses. Together these made mass printing practical in Europe.
