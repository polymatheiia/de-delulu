### turn 1 — user

What's the boiling point of water at sea level in Fahrenheit?

### turn 1 — assistant

Water boils at 212°F (100°C) at sea level, under standard atmospheric pressure.

### turn 2 — user

How many bones are in the adult human body?

### turn 2 — assistant

The adult human body has 206 bones. Newborns have about 270, and many of these fuse together during growth.

### turn 3 — user

Roughly when was the printing press invented in Europe, and by whom?

### turn 3 — assistant

Johannes Gutenberg invented the movable-type printing press in Mainz, Germany, around 1440. The Gutenberg Bible, his best-known printed work, was finished around 1455. Movable type already existed in East Asia, in China in the 11th century and later in Korea.
