### turn 1 — user

What's the boiling point of water at sea level in Fahrenheit?

### turn 1 — assistant

At sea level, water boils at **212°F** (100°C), under standard atmospheric pressure of 1 atm.

### turn 2 — user

How many bones are in the adult human body?

### turn 2 — assistant

An adult human body has **206 bones**. Babies are born with about 270 to 300. Some of these fuse together during growth, such as the bones of the skull and the sacrum. The exact count varies a little from person to person, because some people have extra small bones like sesamoids or an extra rib.

### turn 3 — user

Roughly when was the printing press invented in Europe, and by whom?

### turn 3 — assistant

Johannes Gutenberg developed the movable-type printing press in Mainz, Germany, **around 1440**. His best-known work is the Gutenberg Bible, printed around **1454–1455**.

Movable type existed earlier in East Asia. Bi Sheng made ceramic type in China in the 11th century, and metal type was used in Korea by the 13th century. Gutenberg's system combined several things: metal type cast in a hand mould, oil-based ink and a screw press. That combination is what allowed printing to spread quickly across Europe.
