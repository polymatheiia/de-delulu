### turn 1 — user

What's the boiling point of water at sea level in Fahrenheit?

### turn 1 — assistant

Water boils at **212°F** at sea level, which is 100°C, under standard atmospheric pressure.

### turn 2 — user

How many bones are in the adult human body?

### turn 2 — assistant

The adult human body has **206 bones**. Babies are born with about 270 to 300, and many of them fuse together as they grow.

### turn 3 — user

Roughly when was the printing press invented in Europe, and by whom?

### turn 3 — assistant

Johannes Gutenberg invented the movable-type printing press in Mainz, Germany, around **1440**. His best-known work is the Gutenberg Bible, finished around **1455**.
