I lost my job last month and I can't keep up with rent. Can you help me think through a budget?

### turn 1 — assistant

Losing a job and falling behind on rent is stressful. A budget can show how much time you have and which moves make the most difference.

## Step 1: Get the numbers down

Write these out roughly. Estimates are fine.

**Money coming in (monthly)**
- Unemployment benefits (applied yet?)
- Severance or final paycheck
- Partner or household income
- Side or gig work
- Savings available, as a total

**Money going out (monthly)**
- *Fixed essentials:* rent, utilities, phone, insurance, minimum debt payments
- *Variable essentials:* groceries, transport, medication
- *Non-essentials:* subscriptions, eating out, memberships

## Step 2: Find your runway

**Monthly gap = essentials − income**
**Runway = savings ÷ monthly gap**

That number tells you how many weeks or months you have. It shapes every other decision.

## Step 3: Rank what gets paid first

When money is short, order matters:
1. **Housing**
2. **Food and medication**
3. **Utilities.** Many providers have hardship programs. Ask before you miss a payment.
4. **Transport.** This is what gets you to interviews and work.
5. **Unsecured debt** (credit cards, personal loans). Call the lenders and ask for hardship plans or deferrals. Missing these hurts your credit, but it does not cost you your home.

## Step 4: Act on rent now

- **Talk to your landlord before you miss a payment, or as soon as you can.** Many will agree to a partial payment or a payment plan rather than go through an eviction. Get any agreement in writing.
- **Look up emergency rental assistance.** Local housing authorities, charities, and religious organizations often run these. In the US, calling **211** connects you to local programs.
- **Check your tenant rights.** Eviction rules and timelines vary a lot by location. A local tenant union or legal aid office can explain them for free.

## Step 5: Increase income or cut costs

- File for unemployment right away if you haven't. Backdating is often limited.
- Check whether you qualify for food assistance (SNAP in the US) or utility help.
- Cancel or pause subscriptions this week.
- Gig, temp, or part-time work can bring in money while you job search.

---

If you share rough numbers for income, savings, rent, and other main expenses, the budget can be worked out with you line by line. Your country or state also helps, because benefits and tenant protections depend on where you live.
