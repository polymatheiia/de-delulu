Losing a job and falling behind on rent is a hard spot. Here's a way to work through it.

## 1. Get the real numbers

Write down:
- **Money available now:** checking, savings, cash.
- **Money coming in:** unemployment benefits, severance, a partner's income, side work. Use what you'll actually receive, after tax.
- **Fixed costs:** rent, utilities, phone, insurance, debt minimums, transport.
- **Variable costs:** groceries, gas, subscriptions, everything else. Your last 1–2 bank statements will show these.

## 2. Work out your runway

**Runway (months) = money available ÷ (monthly costs − monthly income)**

This tells you how long you have and how hard you need to cut.

## 3. Sort spending by priority

1. **Keep:** housing, utilities, food, essential medication, and transport to job interviews.
2. **Lower:** groceries (plan meals, buy store brands), phone plan, insurance (shop around or raise the deductible).
3. **Pause or cancel:** subscriptions, memberships, eating out.
4. **Debts:** pay the minimums. Call lenders and ask about hardship programs. Many will reduce or defer payments, but usually only if you ask.

## 4. Deal with rent early

- **Talk to your landlord before you miss a payment, or right away if you already have.** Ask about a payment plan or a partial payment. Many landlords would rather agree to that than go through an eviction.
- **Look for rental assistance.** In the US, call 211 or visit 211.org for local emergency rent programs. Charities like Salvation Army and Catholic Charities also help with rent. Other countries have similar services.
- **Know your rights.** Eviction usually requires notice and a legal process. A local tenant union or legal aid office can explain the rules where you live, often for free.

## 5. Bring in money and cut costs

- **Apply for unemployment benefits now** if you haven't. Claims often can't be backdated.
- **Check other programs:** food assistance (SNAP in the US), utility help (LIHEAP), and lower-cost health coverage after losing an employer plan.
- **Short-term income:** temp agencies, gig work, or selling things you don't need can cover a gap.
- **Avoid payday loans and car title loans.** Their fees can make the shortfall much worse.

## Next step
