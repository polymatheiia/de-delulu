### turn 1 — assistant

Losing a job and falling behind on rent at the same time is a lot of pressure. Here is a way to work through it.

## Step 1: Get the real numbers

Write down:

**Money coming in (monthly)**
- Unemployment benefits (if you've applied)
- Severance, side work, or anything else
- Savings available

**Fixed costs**
- Rent
- Utilities (electric, gas, water, internet, phone)
- Insurance (health, car, renter's)
- Minimum debt payments
- Subscriptions

**Variable costs**
- Groceries
- Transport/gas
- Medications
- Everything else

The key number is **monthly shortfall** = costs − income. Divide your savings by that shortfall to get your **runway** in months.

## Step 2: Sort spending by priority

In a crisis, pay things in this order:
1. Housing and utilities
2. Food and medication
3. Transportation needed for job hunting
4. Insurance (health especially)
5. Secured debts (car loan)
6. Unsecured debts (credit cards, personal loans). These can usually be paused or reduced.

Cancel or pause all subscriptions for now. Credit card companies often have hardship programs if you call and explain.

## Step 3: Handle rent directly

- **Talk to your landlord early**, before you miss a payment if you can. Many landlords will agree to a payment plan or partial payment. Eviction costs them money too.
- **Rental assistance**: In the US, call **211** or visit 211.org. They connect you to local emergency rent, utility, and food programs.
- **Utility assistance**: LIHEAP helps with energy bills. Most utility companies also have hardship plans.
- If you get an eviction notice, look up local **tenant legal aid** right away. Deadlines matter.

## Step 4: Increase income where possible

- Apply for unemployment now if you haven't. Delays cost you weeks of benefits.
- SNAP (food assistance) frees up cash for rent.
- Short-term or gig work can cover the gap while you search.
- Check whether you qualify for Medicaid or marketplace insurance with a reduced income.

## Next step

If you share rough numbers (income, rent, other main bills, savings), a concrete budget with your actual shortfall and runway can be worked out. Let me know your country or state too, because assistance programs vary by location.
