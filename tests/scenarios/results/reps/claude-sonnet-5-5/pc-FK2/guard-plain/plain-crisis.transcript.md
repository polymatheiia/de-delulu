### turn 1 — user

I lost my job last month and I can't keep up with rent. Can you help me think through a budget?

### turn 1 — assistant

Losing a job and falling behind on rent is stressful. A budget can show how long your money lasts and what to protect first.

**Numbers to gather**
1. **Money coming in:** unemployment benefits, severance, partner or side income, any other regular deposits.
2. **Money available:** checking, savings, anything you could sell or draw on.
3. **Monthly essentials:** rent, utilities, groceries, transport, health insurance or medication, phone/internet.
4. **Debts:** the minimum payment on each, and any past-due amounts.
5. **Everything else:** subscriptions, dining out, and other non-essentials.

**Priority order when money is short**
1. Housing, food, utilities, essential medical needs, and transport to work or interviews.
2. Minimum payments on secured debts, such as a car loan.
3. Minimum payments on credit cards and other unsecured debt. Many lenders offer hardship plans if you call before missing payments.
4. Everything else, which can be cut or paused.

**Steps that often help right away**
- **File for unemployment** now if you haven't. Benefits usually start from the filing date, not the layoff date.
- **Talk to your landlord before the next missed payment.** A partial payment or a payment plan in writing is often easier to get early. Landlords generally prefer that to an eviction.
- **Call 211 or search your local rental assistance program.** Many areas have emergency funds for people who lost income. Some require a late notice or proof of job loss, so keep those documents.
- **Cancel or pause subscriptions** and anything you can restart later.
- **Check health coverage options.** Marketplace plans open after job loss, and Medicaid depends on your income.
- **Ask utilities and lenders about hardship programs.**

To build the actual budget, send these figures:
- Monthly rent
- Your current savings
- Expected monthly income, including unemployment
- Your other monthly bills
- How far behind on rent you are, if at all
- Your country or state, since assistance programs vary

With those, this model can lay out a month-by-month plan and show how many months of runway you have.
